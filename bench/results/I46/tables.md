# I46: results

Generated by bench/i46/summarize.py from the JSON files next to this one, and for the start rows and the scales of R from
the inputs of prepare.py. Distances are the mean displacement over the OCT tissue from the authors' registration R, or from
the label-only fit L where named. They measure agreement with these registrations, which are not ground truth. Counts carry
their Clopper-Pearson 95 % interval, which treats the runs of a row as independent. They are not, so the interval describes
the placement of the crops on this one block, not a rate on other specimens. Medians are over all runs of a row, a run
without a transform counted as beyond every other. The vessel check is a median over the runs with at least 50 MRI vessel
labels inside the OCT tissue, counted in brackets.

## Reference

R is the authors' registration, mapping the OCT world to the MRI world in the test's OCT frame. The label-only fit L
(reference_check.py, an affine fitted to the vessels alone) lies 0.155 mm from R on average (p95 0.31 mm, rotation 0.57°),
0.09 / 0.17 / 0.28 mm in the upper, middle and deepest third of the OCT depth. Fitting half of the labels moves L by 0.09 mm
(rms over ten splits of the MRI labels) and 0.09 mm (of the OCT vessels). The 9-parameter fit S9 lies 0.169 mm from R.
Distances below 0.30 mm are therefore not interpreted.

The vessel check gives a median of 0.101 mm at R (2955 labels inside the OCT tissue), 0.211 mm with R shifted by 0.5 mm and
0.247 mm shifted by 1 mm (means over 12 directions). R was fitted with a vessel term of the same kind, so the check favours
R.

Grey/white contrast-to-noise ratio of the images inside the box of the OCT: 10° FLASH 3.49, 20° FLASH 2.87, 30° FLASH 1.98,
T1 map 0.49.
