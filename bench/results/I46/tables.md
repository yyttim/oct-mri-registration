# I46: results

Generated by bench/i46/summarise.py from the JSON files next to this one, and for the start rows and the scales of R from
the inputs of prepare.py. bench/i46/README.md describes the test. Distances are the mean displacement over the OCT tissue
from the authors' registration R, or from the label-only fit L where named. Counts are numbers of runs: the ten crops of a
condition are shifted boxes around the same block. Medians are over all runs of a row. A run that ended without a readable
transform (a tool error) counts as beyond every other.

## Reference

R is the authors' registration, from the OCT world to the MRI world in the test's OCT frame. The label-only fit L
(reference_check.py, an affine fitted to the vessels alone) lies 0.155 mm from R on average (p95 0.31 mm, rotation 0.57°),
0.09 / 0.17 / 0.28 mm in the upper, middle and deepest third of the OCT depth, so distances below 0.30 mm are not
interpreted.

Grey/white contrast-to-noise ratio of the images inside the box of the OCT: 10° FLASH 3.49, 20° FLASH 2.87, 30° FLASH 1.98,
T1 map 0.49.
