# I46: results

Generated by bench/i46/summarize.py from the JSON files next to this one, and for the start rows and the scales of R from
the inputs of prepare.py. bench/i46/README.md describes the test. Distances are the mean displacement over the OCT tissue
from the authors' registration R, or from the label-only fit L where named, and neither is ground truth. Counts carry their
Clopper-Pearson 95 % interval, which describes the placement of the crops on this one block, not a rate on other specimens.
Medians are over all runs of a row. A run that ended without a readable transform (a tool error) counts as beyond every
other.

## Reference

R is the authors' registration, from the OCT world to the MRI world in the test's OCT frame. The label-only fit L
(reference_check.py, an affine fitted to the vessels alone) lies 0.155 mm from R on average (p95 0.31 mm, rotation 0.57°),
0.09 / 0.17 / 0.28 mm in the upper, middle and deepest third of the OCT depth, so distances below 0.30 mm are not
interpreted.

Grey/white contrast-to-noise ratio of the images inside the box of the OCT: 10° FLASH 3.49, 20° FLASH 2.87, 30° FLASH 1.98,
T1 map 0.49.

## Ablation

bench/ablate.py: the method with one element removed or replaced, on the C3 crops of the 10° FLASH, and A4 also on the C3
crops of the 20° and 30° FLASH and the C12 crops of the 10° FLASH. The difference is the variant's distance from R minus the
method's on the same crop. The outline is unused on I46, as the masked OCT has almost no measured outside (at most 0.04 % of
its measured voxels). A13 then scores 0 for every pose, so §3 and §4 keep one search candidate in the header orientation,
16.92 to 19.97 mm from R, and §5 then moves it. A9 changes only the OCT weights of the search, so its differences show the
numerical spread of the method, up to 0.04 mm. A10 adds a two-sided outline to §4 alone, as the search keeps the outline
off.
